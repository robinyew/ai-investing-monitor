## Infrastructure Implication

### Most Bullish Signal
The clearest bullish signal is TSMC raising its 2026 full-year revenue outlook on continued AI chip demand. As the foundry chokepoint upstream of nearly all AI silicon, a guidance raise confirms demand is not decelerating and that CoWoS advanced packaging remains the binding constraint. This cascades positively to HBM (MU, SK hynix's $20B expansion intent), optical (COHR/NVIDIA Texas groundbreaking), and networking (ANET 1.6T). The memory-share-of-capex shift to ~48% by 2027 further reinforces that the AI infrastructure capital cycle is broadening, not topping.

### Neutral / Watch
The Upscale AI Skyhammer scale-up silicon raise and the InP 6-inch wafer supply-wall story are both directionally meaningful but need confirmation — one is an early-stage challenger to NVLink with unproven commercial traction, the other a constraint that could ease faster than expected if 6-inch transitions succeed. Arista's 1.6T launch is a product announcement, not a confirmed order, and is shadowed by reported supply-chain shortages. SMCI's NVIDIA blueprint is real but its share move is partly momentum-driven against persistent cash-flow concerns.

### Weakest Signal
Cerebras' margin disclosure, while confirming AI accelerator demand breadth, primarily reinforces NVIDIA's existing moat rather than introducing a new directional thesis. Numerous market-cap, price-target, and "best stock" articles were excluded as noise.
