This magnitude of spend directly funds GPUs, custom ASICs, networking, memory, and power buildout across every primary ticker, and is corroborated by concrete deployment signals (Vultr GB300, NVIDIA Ethernet share gains) and supply-side tightness (TSMC/Samsung capacity). The capex base validates demand from the top of the funnel down to optical and power components.

### Neutral / Watch
The custom-silicon thread (AVGO/Google TPU through 2031, Amazon Trainium 80% inference cost advantage) and the foundry rebalancing (Samsung wins, Intel 18A-P) point to a structurally shifting accelerator landscape, but the directional impact on NVIDIA versus ASIC players is not yet conclusive — NVIDIA simultaneously gained Ethernet switching leadership. These are competitive realignments to monitor, not yet decisive flips.

### Weakest Signal
The single-customer deal announcements (SharonAI/NVIDIA 72MW Australia, Rumble/Northern Data acquisition) and broad-market capex commentary carry limited standalone weight given undisclosed scale, micro-cap promotional framing, or analyst-only sourcing. The Apple/Intel US-chip partnership (Trump-confirmed, no company detail) and copper-as-AI-trade pieces lack the specificity needed for high-confidence infrastructure conclusions.
