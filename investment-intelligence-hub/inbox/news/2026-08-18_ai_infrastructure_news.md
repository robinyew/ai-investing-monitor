### Most Bullish Signal

Combined with China's NBS-confirmed +81.8% AI equipment surge and Arista's raised AI networking outlook, the data points converge on a clear theme: **bandwidth and connectivity intensity are scaling faster than compute units**. This is a durable, multi-vendor demand signal favoring the optical and Ethernet layers (MRVL, COHR, LITE, FN, CRDO, ANET).

### Neutral / Watch
Nvidia's move to Wall Street financing for AI chips is genuinely important but two-sided — it can extend the demand cycle while simultaneously injecting leverage and counterparty risk into the ecosystem. The August 26 (NVDA) and August 27 (MRVL) earnings prints are the dominant near-term catalysts and will set direction for the entire complex; nothing should be over-interpreted ahead of them.

### Weakest Signal
The SemiAnalysis TPU forecast cut is a notable but unconfirmed third-party estimate; it warrants monitoring as a possible crack in the custom-silicon narrative but is not company-verified. The Micron item, while pointing to real HBM tightness, was driven predominantly by price-target framing and adds little new factual signal.
